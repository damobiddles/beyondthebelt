Our mission

Nobody should have to leave the mats because of money. Your training partners are your people.

Martial arts teach far more than technique. Training partners become a community: someone who notices when you are missing, someone to talk to, a reason to get out of the house. For many people that makes a real difference to their mental health, loneliness, social life and confidence.

But training costs money, and when money gets tight, the mat is often the first thing to go, just when the community matters most.

Beyond the Belt provides financial support to keep people of all ages and backgrounds training, and to help new people through the door. It is funded by the community itself: members who can afford to give, third-party supporters, local businesses and fundraising events at the academy.

The pathway

From first class to giving back

Training is the start. Staying, belonging and eventually helping the next person through the door is what changes lives.

  1. Show up

    A warm welcome, no experience needed and no pressure. The hardest step is walking in.

  2. Keep going

    Support with the cost, so a tough month does not mean giving up training.

  3. Belong

    Training partners who become friends, and a place that notices when you are missing.

  4. Give back

    Helping the next person: encouraging newcomers, volunteering, fundraising.

Our story

Born at RGA Academy

Beyond the Belt was set up by Paul Busby and members of RGA Academy in Marlow. They are coaches, students, parents and friends who have seen first-hand what training partners and community can do for people.

The charity lets them take that further, making sure money is not the reason someone loses their place on the mats.

Two figures, one belt, and a path that leads well beyond the dojo.
